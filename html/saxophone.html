Saxophone


The saxophone is a versatile woodwind instrument celebrated for its bold, expressive and soulful sound. From smooth jazz melodies to powerful orchestral performances, its distinctive tone and wide musical range make it a favorite among musicians worldwide.

Types of Saxophones

Soprano Saxophone

A small, high-pitched saxophone with a bright and expressive tone. It is often used in jazz and solo performances.

  • Price range: from $300 to $5,000+

    • Tenor Saxophone

    A medium-large saxophone with a rich, warm tone. It is widely used in jazz, blues and popular music.

    • Price range: from $800 to $7,000+

    Alto Saxophone

    The most popular saxophone for beginners. It has a balanced sound and a comfortable size, making it suitable for many musical styles.

    • Price range: from $300 to $5,000+

    Baritone Saxophone

    A large saxophone that produces deep, powerful notes. It provides the bass foundation in many jazz and concert ensembles.

    • Price range: from $2,000 to $10,000+
