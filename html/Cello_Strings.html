Cello Strings


Strings define a cello's voice, shaping its tone, response and projection. Choosing the right set can bring out a warmer, brighter or more powerful sound, whether you play in an orchestra or practise at home.

Types of Cello Strings

Steel Core Strings

Durable and stable in tune, with a bright, focused tone. A great everyday choice for students and beginners.

  • Price range: from $60 to $200

Synthetic Core Strings

Offer a warm, rich sound close to gut while staying stable and long-lasting. Popular with intermediate and professional players.

  • Price range: from $80 to $300

Gut Core Strings

A traditional choice with a deep, complex and earthy tone. They take longer to settle and are more sensitive to weather.

  • Price range: from $150 to $500+

Fractional Size Sets (1/2, 3/4)

Strings made for smaller cellos, giving younger players a comfortable feel and a balanced sound.

  • Price range: from $30 to $150
