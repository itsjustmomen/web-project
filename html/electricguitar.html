Electric Guitar


The electric guitar is a popular string instrument known for its powerful and versatile sound. Using electronic pickups and amplification, it can produce a wide range of tones and is commonly used in rock, blues, jazz, metal and many other music genres.



Types of Electric Guitars

Solid-Body Electric Guitar

The most common type of electric guitar. It has a solid body and uses electronic pickups to produce its sound. It is widely used in rock, blues and pop music.

  • Price range: from $300 to $3,000+

Semi-Hollow Body Electric Guitar

A guitar with a partially hollow body that produces a warm and resonant sound. It is commonly used in jazz, blues and rock music.

  • Price range: from $500 to $4,000+

Hollow-Body Electric Guitar

A fully hollow guitar that creates a warm and rich tone. It is especially popular among jazz musicians and players who prefer a more traditional sound.

  • Price range: from $600 to $5,000+

Extended-Range Electric Guitar

An electric guitar with more than the standard six strings, such as a seven or eight-string guitar. It is often used in metal and progressive music for its wider range of notes.

  • Price range: from $500 to $3,500+
