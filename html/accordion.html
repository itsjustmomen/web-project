Accordion


The accordion is a box-shaped free-reed aerophone instrument played by compressing or expanding a bellows while pressing buttons or keys to produce rich, harmonic melodies. It is a cornerstone of folk, polka, tango, and Cajun music worldwide.


Types of Accordions

Piano Accordion

Features a piano-style keyboard on the right-hand manual and bass buttons on the left, popular in popular and folk music.

  • Price range: from $500 to $5,000+

Chromatic Button Accordion

Equipped with button boards on both the right and left sides, offering an extensive range and multi-key capabilities.

  • Price range: from $1,200 to $8,000+

Diatonic Button Accordion

A simpler folk accordion where each button produces different notes depending on whether the bellows is pushed or pulled.

  • Price range: from $300 to $3,000+

Concertina

A compact member of the free-reed family with hexagonal or square ends, played with buttons on both sides.

  • Price range: from $400 to $4,000+
