Harp


The harp is one of the oldest and most enchanting musical instruments in the world. dating back thousands of years, the harp has played an important role in cultures across the world.



Types of Harps

Lever harp

A smaller, lightweight harp that uses individual levers to adjust the pitch of its strings. It is popular among beginners and celtic musicians due to its portability and versatility.

  • Has from 22 to 38 strings
  • Price range: from $1,000 to $5,000

Pedal harp

A compact harp designed to rest on the player's lap. Its small size and simple design make it suitable for casual playing and musical exploration.

  • Has from 15 to 27 strings
  • Price range: from $200 to $1,000+

Electric Harp

A modern harp equipped with electronic pickups, allowing it to be amplified and connected to sound systems. It is often used in contemporary performances and live music.

  • Has from 22 to 47 strings
  • Price range: from $2,000 to $10,000+
