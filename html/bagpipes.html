Bagpipes


Bagpipes are a traditional woodwind instrument using enclosed reeds fed from a constant reservoir of air in a bag. Renowned for their powerful, stirring drone sound, they are iconic symbols of Scottish and Celtic culture.


Types of Bagpipes

Great Highland Bagpipe

The most famous Scottish bagpipe featuring three drones and a powerful, outdoor projecting chanter.

  • Price range: from $800 to $4,000+

Scottish Smallpipes

Mellow, bellows-blown bagpipes designed for indoor play and sessions with other acoustic instruments.

  • Price range: from $700 to $2,500+

Uilleann Pipes

The national bagpipes of Ireland, elbow-blown with regulators that allow the player to play chordal accompaniments.

  • Price range: from $1,500 to $6,000+

Border Pipes

Mouth-blown bagpipes originating from the Anglo-Scottish border region, featuring a louder tone than smallpipes.

  • Price range: from $800 to $2,500+
