Violin


The violin is a timeless string instrument celebrated for its expressive, bright and emotional sound. Its versatility allows it to shine in classical orchestras, folk music and contemporary performances, making it one of the most beloved instruments in the world.

Types of Violins

Acoustic Violin

The traditional violin, known for its warm and resonant sound. It is used in classical music, folk music and orchestras.

  • Price range: from $100 to $10,000+

    • Electric Violin

    A modern violin equipped with electronic pickups that allow it to be amplified. It is popular in rock, pop and experimental music.

    • Price range: from $300 to $5,000+

    Baroque Violin

    A historical violin designed to reflect the instruments used during the Baroque period. It typically features a different setup and produces a distinctive period sound.

    • Price range: from $,000 to $10,000+

    Fractional Violin

    A smaller violin designed for children and young beginners. Available in sizes such as 1/4, 1/2 and 3/4.

    • Price range: from $100 to $1,000+
