Amplifiers


Amplifiers are electronic devices that increase the strength of audio signals, allowing instruments to produce louder sounds. They are essential in concerts, rehearsals and recording studios. Different amplifiers are designed for different instruments and musical styles.



Types of Amplifiers

Electric Guitar Amplifier

Designed specifically for electric guitars. It allows musicians to control volume, tone and distortion, making it popular in rock, metal, blues and other genres.

  • Price range: from $100 to $5,000+

Acoustic Guitar Amplifier

Designed to amplify acoustic and acoustic-electric guitars while maintaining their natural sound. Many models include built-in effects and microphone inputs.

  • Price range: from $150 to $2,000+

Bass Amplifier

Built to reproduce the deep frequencies of bass guitars. It provides powerful low-frequency sound and is commonly used in rehearsals and live performances.

  • Price range: from $150 to $3,000+

Keyboard Amplifier

Designed for keyboards and digital pianos. It reproduces a wide range of frequencies and helps maintain clear sound across different tones.

  • Price range: from $150 to $1,500+

Tube Amplifier

An amplifier that uses vacuum tubes to produce sound. It is especially popular among rock and blues guitarists for its distinctive tone and response.

  • Price range: from $400 to $5,000+

Modelling Amplifier

A digital amplifier that recreates the sounds of different amplifier models and effects. It allows musicians to experiment with many different tones.

  • Price range: from $150 to $2,500+
