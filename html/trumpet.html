Trumpet

The trumpet is a brass wind instrument known for its bright, powerful and distinctive sound. It is commonly used in classical orchestras, jazz bands, marching bands and modern music. Its ability to produce both strong and expressive tones makes it one of the most recognizable brass instruments.

Types of Trumpets

Bb Trumpet

The most common type of trumpet, used by beginners and professionals. It is popular in orchestras, jazz bands and many other musical performances.

  • Price range: from $200 to $5,000+

C Trumpet

Commonly used in orchestras and classical performances. It produces a slightly higher pitch than the Bb trumpet and is especially popular among professional musicians.

  • Price range: from $1,000 to $6,000+

Piccolo Trumpet

A smaller trumpet designed to produce higher notes. It is often used in Baroque music and advanced classical performances.

  • Price range: from $1,500 to $8,000+

Pocket Trumpet

A compact trumpet with tightly coiled tubing. Despite its smaller size, it has a similar playing range to a standard trumpet.

  • Price range: from $150 to $1,500+
