Bass Guitar


The bass guitar is a string instrument known for producing deep, low sounds. It provides the rhythmic and harmonic foundation in many types of music, including rock, jazz, funk and pop.


Types of Bass Guitars

Four-String Bass

The most common type of bass guitar. It is versatile and widely used in many different music styles.

  • Price range: from $200 to $3,000+

Five-String Bass

A bass guitar with an additional low string that provides a wider range of notes and deeper tones.

  • Price range: from $400 to $4,000+

Fretless Bass

A bass without metal frets on the fingerboard. It produces a smooth and expressive sound and allows players to create unique tones.

  • Price range: from $500 to $5,000+

Acoustic Bass Guitar

A bass guitar with a hollow body that produces sound acoustically. It is often used for unplugged performances and acoustic music.

  • Price range: from $300 to $3,000+
