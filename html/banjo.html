Banjo


The banjo is a stringed instrument featuring a thin membrane stretched over a circular frame or cavity as a resonator. Known for its distinct percussive and bright sustain, it is a staple of bluegrass, country, and folk music.


Types of Banjos

Five-String Banjo

The most popular banjo configuration featuring a drone string, essential for bluegrass and clawhammer styles.

  • Price range: from $300 to $3,500+

Tenor Banjo

A four-string banjo with a shorter neck, widely used in traditional Irish traditional music and jazz.

  • Price range: from $350 to $2,500+

Open-Back Banjo

A banjo without a heavy metal tone ring or resonator back, producing a warmer, quieter sound suited for old-time folk music.

  • Price range: from $250 to $2,000+

Plectrum Banjo

A four-string banjo with a longer neck designed to be played with a flatpick, popular in Dixieland jazz.

  • Price range: from $400 to $3,000+
