Speakers


Speakers are audio devices that convert electrical signals into sound. They are used in music studios, concerts, home entertainment systems and professional sound setups. Different speakers are designed for different purposes, from accurate studio audio to powerful live performances.


Types of Speakers

Studio Monitor Speakers

Designed for music production, mixing and recording. They reproduce sound accurately, allowing musicians and producers to hear details in their audio.

  • Price range: from $100 to $3,000+

PA Speakers

Public address speakers are designed for live performances, events and venues. They project sound across large spaces and are commonly used by bands and DJs.

  • Price range: from $200 to $5,000+

Powered Speakers

Speakers with built-in amplifiers, meaning they do not require a separate external power amplifier to operate.

  • Price range: from $100 to $3,000+

Passive Speakers

Speakers that require an external amplifier to produce sound. They are commonly used in professional audio systems and larger sound setups.

  • Price range: from $100 to $4,000+

Subwoofers

Special speakers designed to reproduce very low bass frequencies. They add depth and impact to music and are often used with professional sound systems.

  • Price range: from $150 to $4,000+

Portable Bluetooth Speakers

Compact wireless speakers designed for easy transportation and everyday listening. Many models include rechargeable batteries and Bluetooth connectivity.

  • Price range: from $30 to $1,000+

Floor Monitor Speakers

Speakers placed on the stage floor and directed toward performers. They allow musicians to hear themselves and other instruments during live shows.

  • Price range: from $150 to $2,500+
