Clarinet


The clarinet is a versatile woodwind instrument known for its warm, rich tone and expressive sound. From smooth jazz melodies to classical orchestral performances, it offers a wide range of musical possibilities.

Types of clarinets

B♭ Soprano Clarinet

The most common clarinet, widely used in school bands, jazz ensembles and orchestras. It has a balanced, versatile tone.

  • Price range: from $400 to $5,000+

A Clarinet

Slightly longer than the B♭ clarinet, it produces a warm tone and is commonly used in classical orchestral music.

  • Price range: from $2,000 to $8,000+

E♭ Clarinet

A smaller clarinet with a bright, high-pitched sound. It is often used for featured passages in orchestral and concert band music.

  • Price range: from $1,500 to $5,000+

Bass Clarinet

A larger instrument with a deep, rich sound. Its curved metal neck and bell make it visually distinctive.

  • Price range: from $2,000 to $10,000+

Alto Clarinet

A larger clarinet pitched lower than the standard soprano clarinet. It provides a mellow sound that helps fill out wind ensembles.

  • Price range: from $2,000 to $7,000+
