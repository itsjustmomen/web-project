French Horn


The French horn is a brass instrument made of tubing wrapped into a coiled bell shape, known for its rich, warm, and majestic timbre. It is a vital component of orchestral symphonies and concert band arrangements.


Types of French Horns

Double Horn

The most popular horn combining F and B-flat sides into a single instrument using a thumb valve, offering security in high ranges and deep tone.

  • Price range: from $2,500 to $7,500+

Single Horn

A lighter horn pitched entirely in either F or B-flat, commonly used by younger students before transitioning to a double horn.

  • Price range: from $1,200 to $3,500+

Triple Horn

An advanced professional horn featuring three tuning sets (F, B-flat, and high F) for maximum range security.

  • Price range: from $8,000 to $15,000+

Vienna Horn

A specialized regional horn utilizing piston valves rather than rotary valves, prized for its uniquely blended, warm orchestral sound.

  • Price range: from $6,000 to $12,000+
