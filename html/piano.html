Piano


The piano is a versatile keyboard instrument known for its rich sound and wide range of notes. It is used in classical music, jazz, pop and many other musical styles, making it one of the most popular instruments in the world.


Types of Pianos

Grand Piano

A large piano with a horizontal frame and strings. It produces a powerful and rich sound and is commonly used in concert halls and professional performances.

  • Price range: from $10,000 to $100,000+

Upright Piano

A vertical piano designed to take up less space. It is a popular choice for homes, schools and music studios.

  • Price range: from $3,000 to $15,000+

Digital Piano

A modern electronic piano that recreates the sound and feel of an acoustic piano. It often includes different sounds and features.

  • Price range: from $300 to $5,000+

Baby Grand Piano

A smaller version of a grand piano that offers a rich sound while fitting more easily into homes and smaller spaces.

  • Price range: from $8,000 to $50,000+
