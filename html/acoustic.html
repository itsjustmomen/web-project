Acoustic Guitar

The acoustic guitar is one of the most popular musical instruments. It produces sound through the vibration of its strings, which is amplified by its hollow body. Acoustic guitars are commonly used in rock, pop, folk, country, and many other music genres.

Types of Acoustic Guitars

Dreadnought Acoustic Guitar

Dreadnought guitars have a large body and produce a powerful, full sound. They are commonly used for strumming and playing different music styles.

  • Price range: $150 – $1,500+

Concert Acoustic Guitar

Concert guitars have a smaller body than dreadnought guitars. They offer a balanced sound and are comfortable to play.

  • Price range: $200 – $1,200+

Classical Guitar

Classical guitars use nylon strings and have a softer sound. They are often used for classical music, fingerstyle playing, and traditional music.

  • Price range: $100 – $1,000+

Acoustic-Electric Guitar

Acoustic-electric guitars combine the natural sound of an acoustic guitar with built-in electronics, allowing them to be connected to an amplifier.

  • Price range: $250 – $2,000+
