Acoustic Guitar


The acoustic guitar is a popular string instrument known for its natural, warm sound. It produces music through the vibration of its strings, which is amplified by its hollow wooden body. It is commonly used in rock, pop, folk and country music.

Types of Acoustic Guitars

Dreadnought Acoustic Guitar

A large-bodied acoustic guitar known for its powerful volume and full sound. It is commonly used for strumming and different music styles.

  • Price range: from $150 to $1,500+

Concert Acoustic Guitar

A smaller-bodied guitar that produces a balanced sound and offers comfortable playing, making it suitable for fingerstyle and strumming.

  • Price range: from $200 to $1,200+

Classical Guitar

A guitar that uses nylon strings and produces a soft, warm tone. It is commonly used for classical music and fingerstyle playing.

  • Price range: from $100 to $1,000+

Acoustic-Electric Guitar

An acoustic guitar equipped with electronic pickups, allowing it to be connected to an amplifier while maintaining its natural acoustic sound.

  • Price range: from $250 to $2,000+
