Acoustic Guitar


The acoustic guitar is a string instrument that produces sound naturally through its hollow body. It is popular in folk, pop, country and many other styles of music because of its warm and natural sound.


Types of Acoustic Guitars

Dreadnought Guitar

A large-bodied acoustic guitar known for its powerful sound and strong bass response. It is one of the most popular types of acoustic guitars.

  • Price range: from $200 to $5,000+

Concert Guitar

A smaller acoustic guitar with a balanced sound. Its comfortable size makes it suitable for many different players.

  • Price range: from $300 to $4,000+

Classical Guitar

A nylon-string guitar known for its warm and soft tone. It is commonly used for classical, flamenco and traditional music.

  • Price range: from $150 to $3,000+

Acoustic-Electric Guitar

An acoustic guitar with built-in electronics that allow the instrument to be connected to an amplifier or sound system.

  • Price range: from $300 to $5,000+
