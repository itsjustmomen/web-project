Drums


The drum is a percussion instrument known for its powerful rhythm and energetic sound. Its versatility makes it essential in rock, jazz, pop and orchestral music, driving the beat and bringing energy to every performance.

Types of drums

Acoustic Drum Kit

The classic drum set, usually including a bass drum, snare, toms and cymbals. It produces a full, powerful sound and is widely used in bands, studios and live performances.

  • Price range: from $400 to $5,000+

Electronic Drum Kit

A modern drum set that uses electronic pads and a sound module. It offers adjustable volume and a wide range of sounds, making it ideal for practice and recording.

  • Price range: from $300 to $4,000+

Snare Drum

A single drum with a crisp, sharp tone, commonly used in marching bands, orchestras and drum kits.

  • Price range: from $80 to $1,500+

Cajón

A box-shaped percussion instrument played by hand. It has a warm, punchy sound and is popular for acoustic and unplugged performances.

  • Price range: from $50 to $500
