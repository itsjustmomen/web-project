Mandolin


The mandolin is a stringed musical instrument of the lute family usually plucked with a plectrum. It produces a bright, shimmering, and percussive melodic sound that is essential to bluegrass, folk, and classical ensemble music.


Types of Mandolins

A-Style Mandolin

Featuring a teardrop-shaped body, known for a clean, balanced tone and commonly used in folk, classical, and celtic music.

  • Price range: from $200 to $2,500+

F-Style Mandolin

Recognizable by its ornate scroll and points, favored by bluegrass musicians for its punchy, projecting chop and tone.

  • Price range: from $500 to $5,000+

Bowl-Back (Neapolitan) Mandolin

A historical mandolin featuring a rounded, bowl-shaped back constructed from curved wooden staves, traditional in classical mandolin music.

  • Price range: from $400 to $3,000+

Resonator Mandolin

A mandolin outfitted with one or more aluminum cones to amplify acoustic projection, popular in blues and roots music.

  • Price range: from $500 to $2,000+
