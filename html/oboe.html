Oboe


The oboe is a double-reed woodwind instrument celebrated for its penetrating, expressive, and distinctive tone. Its versatility allows it to shine in classical orchestras, wind ensembles, and chamber music, making it a foundational voice in the woodwind family.


Types of Oboes

Conservatory Oboe

The standard model used worldwide, featuring a full conservatory key system with a rich and versatile tone.

  • Price range: from $2,500 to $12,000+

Thumbplate Oboe

A variant popular primarily in the UK, utilizing a thumbplate key system for alternative fingerings.

  • Price range: from $3,000 to $10,000+

English Horn (Cor Anglais)

A larger double-reed instrument pitched a fifth lower than the standard oboe, known for its dark, melancholic voice.

  • Price range: from $5,000 to $15,000+

Student Oboe

A simplified oboe built with resin or simplified key systems designed for durability and young beginners.

  • Price range: from $1,000 to $3,000+
