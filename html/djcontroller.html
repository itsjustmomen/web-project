DJ Controllers


DJ controllers are audio devices that let DJs control music software using physical jog wheels, mixer controls and performance pads. They are used for practicing, recording mixes and performing live.


Types of DJ Controllers

2-Channel Controllers

Compact controllers with two mixer channels are a popular choice for learning to mix and for smaller setups. They commonly include two decks, a crossfader and headphone cue controls.

  • Price range: from $100 to $600

4-Channel Controllers

These controllers provide four mixer channels for mixing more tracks or connecting additional audio equipment. They are useful for more advanced and flexible setups.

  • Price range: from $400 to $2,000+

Performance Controllers

Designed for hands-on performance, these models often include responsive jog wheels, performance pads, effects controls and expanded mixer features.

  • Price range: from $300 to $2,000+

Standalone DJ Systems

Standalone systems can play music from supported media without needing a computer during a performance. Many include a built-in display and a full set of mixing controls.

  • Price range: from $800 to $4,000+
