Acoustic Guitar Strings


Acoustic guitar strings bring out the natural sound of the guitar, from warm, mellow chords to bright, ringing picking. The right set can change both how your guitar sounds and how it feels to play.

Types of Acoustic Guitar Strings

Phosphor Bronze Strings

The most popular choice, with a warm, balanced tone and good durability. Suitable for strumming and fingerpicking.

  • Price range: from $6 to $20

80/20 Bronze Strings

A bright, crisp and lively sound that stands out in a mix. The tone mellows as the strings age.

  • Price range: from $6 to $18

Coated Strings

Wrapped in a thin protective layer that resists sweat and corrosion, so they last several times longer.

  • Price range: from $12 to $25

Nylon Strings (Classical)

Soft, mellow strings for classical and flamenco guitars. Gentle on the fingertips, ideal for beginners.

  • Price range: from $8 to $30
