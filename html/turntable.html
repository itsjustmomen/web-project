Turntables

Enjoy the hands-on experience and rich sound of playing records. Learn about the main turntable types and features before choosing a setup.

Types of Turntables

Belt-Drive Turntables

A motor turns the platter using a belt. Belt-drive models are popular for home listening and are available in options for both beginners and experienced listeners.

Direct-Drive Turntables

The motor drives the platter directly, allowing it to start quickly and maintain speed. This design is commonly chosen by DJs and people who want responsive playback.

Manual Turntables

The listener moves the tonearm onto the record and lifts it off at the end. Manual models offer a simple, hands-on listening experience.

Automatic Turntables

Automatic models move the tonearm into position and return it when playback finishes, making them convenient for relaxed home listening.
