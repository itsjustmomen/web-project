Cello


The cello is a classical string instrument renowned for its deep, warm and expressive sound. Its rich tonal range makes it a beloved instrument in orchestras, chamber music and solo performances, bringing emotion and depth to every melody.

Types of Cellos

Full-Size Cello (4/4)

The standard cello size, designed for adult musicians. It produces a deep, resonant tone and is commonly used in orchestras and solo performances.

  • Price range: from $1,000 to $10,000+

3/4 Cello

A smaller cello commonly used by younger students and musicians who need a more comfortable instrument size.

  • Price range: from $500 to $3,000

1/2 Cello

A half-size cello designed for younger beginners. It maintains the cello's characteristic sound while being easier to handle.

  • Price range: from $300 to $1,500

Electric Cello

A modern cello that uses electronic pickups to amplify its sound. It is suitable for contemporary music and amplified performances.

  • Price range: from $1,000 to $5,000+
