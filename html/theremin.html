Theremin


The theremin is an electronic musical instrument controlled without physical contact by the performer. Featuring two metal antennas that sense the relative position of the hands, it produces eerie, haunting glides popular in sci-fi soundtracks and avant-garde music.


Types of Theremins

Standard Etherwave Theremin

The classic design featuring vertical pitch and horizontal volume antennas, engineered for accurate professional performance.

  • Price range: from $400 to $1,000+

Compact / Desktop Theremin

A simplified, portable electronic theremin built for hobbyists and educational sound exploration.

  • Price range: from $150 to $400+

Vacuum Tube Theremin

A boutique vintage-circuit theremin using traditional glass vacuum tubes for a warm, authentic retro tone.

  • Price range: from $1,000 to $3,500+
