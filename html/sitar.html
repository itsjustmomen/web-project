Sitar


The sitar is a plucked string instrument originating from the Indian subcontinent, famous for its resonant, buzzing sympathetic strings and deep spiritual sound in Hindustani classical music and fusion performances.


Types of Sitars

Vilayat Khan Style Sitar

A streamlined sitar with fewer sympathetic strings and a simpler carved gourd, favored for instrumental gayaki ang styles.

  • Price range: from $800 to $4,000+

Ravi Shankar Style Sitar

A fully-decorated double-tumbada sitar with rich ornamentation and an expanded set of sympathetic strings for deep resonance.

  • Price range: from $1,000 to $6,000+

Bass Sitar (Surbahar)

A larger, deeper cousin of the sitar designed for slow, meditative alap movements in classical Indian music.

  • Price range: from $1,500 to $5,000+
